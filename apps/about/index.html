NEON OS

A desktop of music apps in the Neon Orbit style, made by Madoromi. Everything you add stays on this device: songs are read from your own folders or kept in this browser, and nothing is uploaded.

Updates

Neon OS updates itself from the website: changes download in the background and are used the next time it starts. A new desktop app (the window around Neon OS) is only needed now and then; this says when one is out.
